AppLock

Lock and hide ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini chats.

Your recovery code

If you forget your password, this code lets you set a new one. It is shown only once. Save it in your password manager or write it down.

Forgot your password?

Set a new password with Touch ID on this Mac or with your recovery code. Your locked chats stay locked.

Without Touch ID or a recovery code the password can’t be reset. Removing the extension makes your locked chats visible again.

1 · Choose a password

2 · Touch ID

Unlock with your fingerprint instead of typing the password.

3 · Recovery code

A recovery code lets you reset a forgotten password.

4 · Behaviour

Shortcuts: Option+Shift+L locks the open chat · Option+Shift+K hides everything again. You can also right-click any chat in the sidebar.

Hide on your phone too

Archived chats leave the chat list in every ChatGPT app — iPhone, Android, Mac and the web — not just this browser. Removing the lock in AppLock puts the chat back. Archiving isn’t a lock: anyone using your ChatGPT app can still open Settings › Archived chats, so also turn on Face ID for the ChatGPT app on your phone. Claude and Gemini have no archive, so their chats stay visible in their phone apps.

Other computers and your phone

When you’re signed in to Chrome with sync on, your locks and password follow you to Chrome on your other computers. Install AppLock there too. Only chat IDs sync, never titles. Touch ID is set up separately on each Mac.

No extension can change the ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini phone apps. On iPhone, lock the whole app instead: touch and hold its icon, then choose Require Face ID.
