AppLock

Lock and hide ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini chats on this browser.

1 · Choose a password

2 · Touch ID

Unlock with your fingerprint instead of typing the password.

3 · Behaviour

Shortcuts: Alt+Shift+L locks the open chat · Alt+Shift+K hides everything again. You can also right-click any chat in the sidebar.

Locked chats still exist on the provider's servers and on your other devices. AppLock hides them in this browser profile. Forgot the password? Remove and reinstall the extension; locked chats simply become visible again.
